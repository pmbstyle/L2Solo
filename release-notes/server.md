💰 **Bot economy**

- NPC shops and configured merchants keep their base prices at every progression rate.
- Look for C-, B- and A-grade weapons and armor at dwarven private stores and select city merchants. Dwarven crafters can stock Soulshots, Spiritshots and Blessed Spiritshots up to their crafting grade in any town. Spoilers supply Weapon Enchant Scrolls.
- Bots weigh gear upgrades against the Adena they have available after reserving money for supplies. They judge whether shots are worth buying at the price on offer.
- Bots plan potion and shot supplies around how long it takes to fill their bags. They sell loot and restock in town, or clear low-value items from a full bag when that lets them keep hunting.
- Bots use updated hunting data to choose grounds that suit their level and gear. Background hunts now account for time, supplies and rewards up to events such as a full bag or a death.

⚔️ **Clan parties and raids**

- With an updated C4 client, `.b` opens a single **Party** window for companion controls, recruitment and friends. Use `.bp` to open Search or `.bf` to open Friends. Clan leaders can create additional parties and manage their rosters and movement orders from the same window.
- Clan leaders can command several bot parties against the same raid boss. The parties share targets and use the main party's loot rules. Each party handles its own loot, healing, buffs and resurrection.
- Clan leaders can use the **Saved** tab or `.partysave` and `.partyrestore` to save and reassemble their main and additional parties, including after a server restart. Restoring shows how many members joined and names anyone unavailable.
- Autonomous clans can field raid expeditions of up to 40 bots. Companions in player-led raids stay with their party during combat.
- During raids, support bots can use Recharge on low-mana healers and ranged damage dealers in their own party while prioritizing healing.
- Party companions no longer leave the party in the field to buy shots or visit the newbie buffer; they restock when you bring the party to town. Regroup now recalls companions on a town errand, and the Regroup button still brings them to your side.
- Queen Ant, Core and Orfen now use C4 encounter mechanics, including guards, healers, Orfen's retreat and pull, and their original respawn windows. Player parties and autonomous clans can fight them for epic jewelry. The base drop chance is 30%. Adjust it in the launcher settings independently of the progression preset.

🛠️ **Crafting, combat and travel**

- Helvetia in Giran now sells A- and S-grade gemstones. Clan crafting can hand off ready components correctly, and World Observer shows which materials are ready.
- Mages favor usable spells already in range and check for Cursed Bone before choosing Death Spike.
- Phantom Summoners get the correct C4 Nightshade levels and stats. Curse of Shade, Mass Curse of Shade and Shade Sacrifice now work through the servitor controls.
- Summons and pets are protected from attacks and skills that fail the owner's PvP rules. Damage over time stops at 1 HP.
- Bots without an escape scroll can use mapped walking routes between hunting grounds and town. Bots approach service NPCs independently.
- Improved character switching and recovery when returning to the world.

🗺️ **World Observer and clan AI**

- World Observer shows C4 item icons, links to raid boss details and lets you filter bosses by level.
- If AI title management is enabled, clans can generate titles for several members in one request.
- The **Appearance** tab in World Observer can generate four crest options from a prompt, with an optional colored monogram, for player-managed clans at level 3 and above. Enable it in the launcher settings. The server downloads the 272 MB model on its next start and generates crests locally.
