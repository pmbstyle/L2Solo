⚔️ **Clan progression and weapon upgrades**

- Level 4 clan leaders can start the quest **Pursuit of Clan Ambition!** with Sir Gustav Athebaldt in Oren. Take the Seal of Aspiration and 3,500,000 SP to a Clan Manager to reach clan level 5. Eligible autonomous bot clans can complete the quest too.
- Bots level 40 and above can start **Enhance Your Weapon**, grow a Soul Crystal while hunting, and visit a blacksmith to add a Special Ability to a compatible C- or B-grade weapon. The weapon keeps its enchant level.
- Clan bots can draw unreserved shots and dyes from the clan warehouse before spending Adena. They can buy the remainder through their usual shops or market offers.

💰 **Markets and supplies**

- City merchant prices, fixed buy offers and crafting fees now scale with the server's Adena rate. Regular NPC shops and buybacks use C4 prices.
- Look for C-, B- and A-grade gear on the market board from dwarven crafters, spoilers and stationary traders. These suppliers resell gear from the city reserve. Bots sell loot through the board and use ordinary NPC buybacks for the rest.
- Suppliers stock gear and post material bids based on what bots in each town are ready to buy. Sold-out listings prompt a restock, and gear prices reflect what bots earn while hunting and what nearby sellers charge.
- Dion is the hub for D-grade suppliers and Giran for C-grade and above. Other suppliers follow where bots shop. Gludin and Heine now have designated market locations.
- Dwarven crafters replenish the graded Soulshots, Spiritshots and Blessed Spiritshots they can make at a consistent price for each shot across towns. Bots fund shot refills ahead of gear purchases, and raid parties use the same supply market.
- Bot private stores now follow C4 limits. They get three sell slots and four buy slots, one extra in each for dwarves, plus any slots from Expand Trade.

🤝 **Trade meetings and companions**

- Eligible members of autonomous hunting parties can meet players to trade when the group can spare them. Their party holds their slot so they can return after the trade completes or is cancelled.
- Bots with karma arrange trade meetings in Floran Village and travel there on foot. The market board filters out offers the merchant cannot reach. Map tracking now works for meeting points with fractional coordinates.
- Player companions unload their bags, sell surplus and restock shots when they share a town with their leader. In the field, they ask for a town stop as they approach their inventory slot or carry weight limit.

💬 **Bot dialogue and memory**

- With AI dialogue enabled, bots can check their bags, equipment, skills, party, clan duties and current goals before answering. Ask where an item drops or can be spoiled to get sources from the C4 game data.
- A bot you speak to in person can describe nearby characters, monsters and confirmed threats it can see. Bots can also recall recorded events such as shared hunts, trades, raid results and PvP encounters, including who took part.
